# PadKey Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

PadKey is a Chrome extension that turns your keyboard and mouse into a virtual game controller on Xbox Cloud Gaming (`play.xbox.com`).

## What PadKey collects

Nothing. PadKey does not collect, store, sell, or send any personal data, browsing history, or usage data.

## What PadKey stores

Only your PadKey settings (auto on, mode, mouse sensitivity, on-screen badge). They are saved with `chrome.storage.sync`, which Chrome keeps in your own browser and, if you use Chrome sync, in your Google account. PadKey never sends them anywhere else.

## Where PadKey runs

Only on `https://play.xbox.com/*` and `https://www.xbox.com/*/play*`. There, it reads your key presses and mouse movement inside that tab to drive the virtual controller. Key presses are not recorded or sent anywhere.

## Network

PadKey makes no network requests and loads no remote code.
